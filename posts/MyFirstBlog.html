Welcome to My Blog

Hello world! Welcome to Golnaz.RoughCut, my personal space on the internet where I'll be sharing thoughts, ideas, and reflections on topics that matter to me.

Why "RoughCut"?

A rough cut is the first edited version of a film—unpolished, raw, but full of potential. That's exactly what this blog is: a place for unfiltered ideas, works in progress, and honest explorations of topics I'm passionate about.

What to Expect

Here you'll find my thoughts on:

  • Technology and its impact on our lives
  • Content creation and storytelling
  • Lessons learned from my journey
  • Books, ideas, and things that inspire me
